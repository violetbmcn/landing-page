Sign Club. An app that actually watches how you sign. Learn Auslan with real feedback.

Most apps just show you videos and hope for the best. Sign Club uses your device camera and local computer vision models to check your hand positioning, orientation, and movement pathing in real time.

01 / Camera Analysis

Our model works on-device to map your hand configurations directly against standard Auslan variations. You get immediate spatial feedback without video lag.

02 / Localized Context

Built specifically around Australian Sign Language conventions, including dialect variations across states. No generic globalized substitutions.

03 / Edge Execution

Frames are evaluated completely within local device memory buffers. Your video data never hits a third-party server or cloud storage bucket.
